Oishy Saha

Hi, I am Oishy Saha. Welcome to my profile! I am a fourth year Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Maryland, College Park. I am currently working as a Graduate Research Assistant at the DSPCAD Lab under Dr. Shuvra S. Bhattacharyya.

My research interests broadly span computer vision, edge computing, audio and speech processing, embedded systems, and wireless communication systems. I develop real-time intelligent systems for resource-constrained environments, with applications in additive manufacturing, multimodal audio-video analytics for public safety, and industrial IoT. My long-term goal is to design intelligent, resource-efficient machine learning systems that operate reliably on constrained hardware and enable real-time decision-making at the edge.

My past research focused on affective computing, biomedical signal processing, and medical image analysis. Before joining UMD, I served as a Lecturer in the Department of Electrical, Electronic and Communication Engineering at the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST). I received my B.Sc. degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) in 2022. I am always interested in opportunities to collaborate on efficient AI, audio and speech processing, hardware acceleration, and real-time intelligent systems.
